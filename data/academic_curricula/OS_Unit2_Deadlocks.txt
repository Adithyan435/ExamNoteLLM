Course: Operating Systems
Institution: Universal
Level: Undergraduate
Unit: Unit 2 - Process Scheduling and Deadlocks

Syllabus Scope:
- Process Control Block (PCB), Context switching mechanism, Process states.
- CPU Scheduling algorithms: FCFS, SJF (Preemptive SRTF), Round Robin, Priority Scheduling, Multilevel Queue.
- Deadlock characterization: 4 Coffman conditions (Mutual Exclusion, Hold and Wait, No Preemption, Circular Wait).
- Resource Allocation Graphs (RAG).
- Deadlock Avoidance: Banker's Algorithm (Safety algorithm and Resource-Request algorithm).
